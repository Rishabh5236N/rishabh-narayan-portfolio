I'm Rishabh Narayan, an aspiring Creative Advertiser & Brand Marketer with a significant interest in entertainment & content creation. Comm 494 Research Project Report

Overview. This supervised undergraduate research semester connected market research, advertising strategy, and AI media development. The work centered on three experiences: direct LLM application, deepfake study framing/pretesting, and lab-group feedback across developing media effects studies.

  • Broadened market research knowledge for a future thesis direction.
  • Connected AI integration to media strategy and advertising applications.
  • Built from prior psychology research experience with SONA and Qualtrics-style sampling.

Research tasks. The semester included study parameter framing, survey review, literature review, media analysis, prompt engineering, priming, and feedback across several research projects.

  • Reviewed prior deepfake and AI integration research for project framing.
  • Helped create two full-scale deepfake products for testing.
  • Participated in weekly lab meetings and smaller project testing sessions.

Development process. The final demo required technical iteration across Pinokio, Seedance, Higgsfield, and Adobe Express while working around generation limits, storage constraints, and authenticity issues.

  • Tested many generations to reduce choppiness, timing mismatch, and speech-speed issues.
  • Segmented scripts into concise parts that could fit short generation windows.
  • Used editing, voice timing, and background cleanup to make the materials feel seamless.

Strategic takeaway. The project sharpened study design, literature review, media/content analysis, and presentation skills while pointing toward future thesis work on color theory, branding, AI perception, and advertising campaigns.

  • Learned how research output becomes usable creative and testing material.
  • Practiced translating complex findings into digestible presentations.
  • Built stronger instincts for how audiences understand AI-driven media.
